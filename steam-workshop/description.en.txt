Bring an AI along for the climb. You play your character; on the same PC, the AI plays a second one. Use your own model (DeepSeek, SiliconFlow, ChatGPT, or something running on your computer). Subscribing does not give you an account or an API key.

[b]This mod is still in development.[/b] Some things may be unfinished or break. Please send suggestions and anything that goes wrong — that feedback really helps. Post here: [url=https://github.com/CharTyr/STS2-Agent/issues]GitHub Issues[/url]

[h2]How to start[/h2]
After you subscribe, you do not copy any files.
[olist]
[*]Subscribe and wait for Steam to finish downloading.
[*]In Steam click Play and choose [b]Play with Mods[/b]. Launching the exe directly skips mods.
[*]The first launch may warn that the code is untrusted. Accept it, then fully quit the game and start it again.
[*]In Mods, turn on [b]STS2 AI Agent[/b] and restart once more.
[*]In game, press [b]F8[/b] or the [b]AI[/b] tab on the right.
[*]Open Settings and fill in three things: the model web address, the model name, and your key. If you use Ollama or LM Studio on this PC, the key can stay empty.
[*]Want the AI to help with [b]your[/b] character? Use Chat or Play.
[*]Want a teammate? Go back to the [b]main menu[/b], open [b]AI teammate[/b], and invite. A second window appears. The room has 4 seats; you two take two of them, so friends can still join. The AI only plays its own character.
[/olist]

If you used to copy this mod into the game folder by hand, delete those files and keep only the Workshop subscription so it does not show up twice.

[h2]What you get[/h2]
[list]
[*]Set up your model and chat without leaving the game
[*]Let it play on its own, or take one step at a time
[*]Invite an AI teammate on the same PC
[*]No new cards, no balance tweaks, no changes to your save
[/list]

Needs Slay the Spire 2 [b]v0.111.0[/b] or newer.
